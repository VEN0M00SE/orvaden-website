Privacy

Privacy, by design.

ORVADEN builds focused software that minimizes unnecessary data collection and keeps information under the user's control.

Effective August 23, 2026

Our approach

Privacy requirements depend on what a product actually does. ORVADEN's approach is to collect only what a feature genuinely requires, use local storage where practical, and explain any connected service before it is introduced.

Local-first where it makes sense. Local-first software can reduce unnecessary exposure and give users direct control over their working data. It does not remove the need to protect exported files, device backups, or information shared with other services.

What ORVADEN does not do

  • ORVADEN does not sell personal information from its current products.
  • Current products do not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • ORVADEN does not claim ownership of records users create in its applications.

Product privacy notices

Orvaden Check and Orvaden Vault are local-first products. ORVADEN does not receive the working records stored in either app unless a user voluntarily provides information in a support request.

  • Orvaden Check privacy notice
  • Orvaden Vault privacy notice

Exports and third parties

When a user exports or shares a report, backup, photo, or other file, the selected destination, storage provider, email service, device backup service, or recipient controls what happens to that copy. Users should protect exported information according to their organization's requirements.

Support communications

If you contact ORVADEN, we receive the information you choose to provide in your message. Do not send confidential operational records, photographs, signatures, or backup files unless they are necessary and you are authorized to share them.

Changes to this page

This page may be updated as ORVADEN products and services change. Product-specific notices will remain available where more detailed information is appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@orvaden.com.
