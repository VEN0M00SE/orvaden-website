Orvaden Collision

Responsible use.

Collision is designed to help capture and organize roadside crash notes. It does not replace official reporting, records, evidence, or agency systems.

Effective November 15, 2026

What Collision is

Orvaden Collision is a digital crash field notebook. It is intended to help an authorized user capture, organize, and retrieve information while preparing the official record required by the user's organization.

What Collision is not

Collision is not an official crash report, records-management system, CAD or dispatch system, citation system, evidence-management platform, chain-of-custody system, crash-reconstruction system, or state crash-submission service. It does not determine whether a report is complete, approved, filed, or legally sufficient.

User and agency responsibilities

You are responsible for using Collision in accordance with applicable law, agency policy, public-records and retention requirements, information-security rules, and any restrictions governing personally identifiable or criminal-justice information. Only enter, retain, export, or share information you are authorized to handle.

ORVADEN does not determine whether a particular record, photograph, note, or export is an official record, evidence, or a record subject to a specific retention requirement for your organization.

Review scanned and decoded information

VIN recognition, license-plate recognition, driver's-license barcode parsing, location coordinates, and decoded vehicle information can be incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable. Review important information against the original source before relying on it. Collision is designed to keep manual correction available.

Reference photos

Collision refers to captured images as photos or reference photos. The app does not establish evidentiary status, chain of custody, authenticity, retention classification, or admissibility. If an image must be handled as evidence or an official agency record, follow the applicable agency process and approved system.

Completed status

Marking a Collision record Completed means only that the user has finished collecting notes in the app. It does not mean the official crash report is completed, submitted, approved, accepted by a state system, or closed in an RMS.

Exports and sharing

When you share or export information from Collision, the copy leaves the app's local protection and becomes subject to the destination system, recipient, and your organization's handling requirements. Confirm the destination before sharing sensitive information.

Operational limitations

Collision is not an emergency communications tool and is not a substitute for CAD, dispatch, radio, emergency notification, or any required official system. Do not depend on Collision as the sole repository for information your organization requires to be retained elsewhere.

Changes

This notice may be updated as Collision changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Questions

For product questions, visit Orvaden Collision Support. For privacy questions, review the Orvaden Collision privacy notice or email privacy@orvaden.com.
