Review the Responsible Use notice for the app's intended role and important limitations.

Before contacting support

  1. Record the Orvaden Collision version and build number shown in the app.
  2. Confirm the needed camera, photo, location, or Face ID permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  3. Restart the app and reproduce the issue once, noting the exact steps and any message shown.
  4. Do not uninstall Collision as a troubleshooting step if the device contains records you are required to keep. ORVADEN cannot remotely recover local-only records.

VIN, tag, and driver's-license scanning

Scanning is designed to assist data entry, not replace review. Use a clean, well-lit view, keep the target in focus, and review every recognized value before relying on it. Manual entry remains available when recognition is incomplete or incorrect.

  • VIN: confirm the 17-character result before using it.
  • License plate: review the recognized plate text and enter the issuing state manually when needed.
  • Driver's license: supported PDF417/AAMVA barcode data is parsed on-device. If the barcode cannot be read, use manual entry.

VIN decoding

Confirmed VINs may be decoded through NHTSA's vPIC service to populate vehicle information. VIN decoding requires network access, but a connection is never required to save the crash record or enter year, make, and model manually. If decoding fails, keep the confirmed VIN and continue the record.

Coordinates

Collision requests location only when you tap Get Coordinates. If coordinates cannot be captured, confirm Location Services are enabled for Collision and try again. The app does not continuously track location, and manual location text remains available.

Reference photos

Photos taken in Collision stay in app-managed local storage by default. You may manually share selected photos through the iOS share sheet. A shared copy is controlled by the destination you choose and remains separate from the local Collision copy.

Face ID app lock

If Face ID does not complete, confirm Face ID is configured on the device and permitted for Orvaden Collision. iOS performs the biometric check; ORVADEN does not receive your biometric data.

Saved records and deletion

Collision auto-saves meaningful record changes locally. Permanent deletion removes the selected local record and its associated managed photo files. Deletion is not a cloud-trash feature and cannot be undone by ORVADEN.

What to include in a support request

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The Orvaden Collision version and build number.
  • The feature you were using.
  • The steps immediately before the issue appeared.
  • The exact error message, if any.
  • A screenshot only after sensitive information has been removed.

Do not send driver's-license images, VIN lists, crash records, photographs containing sensitive information, or other personally identifiable information unless it is necessary, you are authorized to share it, and support specifically requests it.
